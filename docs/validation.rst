Validation
==========

On a state-vector emulator, the ported circuits reproduce the classical QLB solver to
machine precision. The figure of merit is the maximum absolute density deviation
:math:`\max|\Delta\rho|` over all sites and recorded times; the state fidelity is 1 to
twelve digits throughout.

.. list-table:: Circuit vs. classical solver
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 34 26 24 16

   * - Test
     - Lattice (qubits)
     - Physics
     - :math:`\max|\Delta\rho|`
   * - 1D free / barrier / phase
     - :math:`2^6` line (8)
     - massless & massive
     - :math:`3.7\times10^{-12}`
   * - 2D oblique Klein
     - :math:`2^5\times2^5` (12)
     - massless barrier
     - :math:`4.7\times10^{-16}`
   * - 3D diagonal mover
     - :math:`2^4\times2^4\times2^4` (14)
     - massless free
     - :math:`1.0\times10^{-17}`
   * - 3D reflecting box
     - :math:`2^5\times2^5\times2^5` (17)
     - massless, bounce-back
     - :math:`6.7\times10^{-17}`

Three dimensions: reflecting box
--------------------------------

An oblique massless packet launched from a corner of a :math:`32^3` box bounces off all
three reflecting walls and returns; the circuit (cyan contours) matches the classical
scheme (filled) to :math:`6.7\times10^{-17}`, with probability conserved.
